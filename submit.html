Your Story Is Part of This Story

The Logan Fire wasn't just about what burned — it was about how people responded. Neighbors helping neighbors. Strangers from cities and small towns sending hay, money, and letters of support. Firefighters, volunteers, families, and people with full hearts who showed up in whatever way they could.

If you were part of this — in any way — we want to hear from you.

Not sure where to start? Consider these questions:

  • How did you hear about the fire?
  • What made you want to help?
  • Who showed up for you — or who did you show up for?
  • What moment still stays with you?
  • What did this experience teach you about people?
