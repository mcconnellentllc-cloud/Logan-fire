We Want to Hear From You

Were you there on March 6, 2017? Did you fight the fire, evacuate your family, lose livestock, or help with recovery? Every story adds another piece to the picture of what our community went through — and how we came together.

Not sure where to start? Consider these questions:

  • Where were you when you first saw the smoke?
  • What do you remember most vividly?
  • Who showed up for you — or who did you show up for?
  • What moment still stays with you?
  • What did you lose? What did you find?
