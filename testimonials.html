Voices of the Fire

The fear was real. The fire was relentless. But so were the people who faced it.

These are the words of people who watched the sky turn black, who felt the heat on their faces, who fought back with everything they had. Farmers, firefighters, volunteers — from every direction they came. This is what they saw. This is what they did. Names have been changed for privacy, but every word is real.
