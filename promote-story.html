From the Bottom of Our Hearts — Thank You

We are deeply grateful that you've taken the time to learn about the Logan Fire. This story belongs to an entire community — the firefighters who stood against walls of flame, the farmers who grabbed shovels and refused to back down, the neighbors who showed up with water trucks and sandwiches and hope.

By sharing this story, you become part of something bigger. You help ensure that the courage, sacrifice, and resilience of these plains people isn't forgotten. You help carry their story forward.

That means everything to us.

Why Your Share Matters

Preserve the Memory

Stories fade without voices to carry them. When you share, you help preserve the memory of March 6, 2017 — the fear, the fight, and the incredible community response.

Honor Those Who Stood

Every firefighter, farmer, and volunteer who faced those flames deserves to be remembered. Your share is a small act of recognition for their courage.

Connect More Voices

Someone you know might have been there. Someone might have a story they've never told. Your share could be the spark that brings another voice forward.

Support the Book

We're documenting these stories in a book to preserve them forever. Every share helps us reach more people who lived through it — and more readers who want to honor them.
