Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Effective Date: February 3, 2026

Introduction

The Logan Fire project ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website www.flamesoffury.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the site.

Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect on the Site includes:

Personal Data You Provide

When you submit a story or sign up for book preorder notifications, we collect:

  • Name: Your real name (kept confidential) and optional alias for publication
  • Email Address: To contact you about your submission or preorder
  • Story Content: The stories and experiences you share with us
  • Photos: Any images you choose to upload with your submission

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages you visit on our site
  • Time and date of your visit
  • Time spent on pages

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Document and preserve the history of the Logan Fire for educational purposes
  • Include your story (with your permission) in our book and website
  • Contact you regarding your submission or preorder
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Protect the security and integrity of our website

Protection of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

Important: Your real name is kept confidential and is only shared with the author. All names in the published book are changed to protect privacy, unless you specifically request to use your real name.

Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Airtable: To securely store form submissions (stories and preorders)
  • Cloudinary: To host uploaded photos
  • YouTube: Embedded videos remain on YouTube's platform

These third-party services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review their policies.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we have about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information and submitted content
  • Withdraw consent for us to use your story at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us:

Email: fire@flamesoffury.com
