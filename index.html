The Logan Fire

When the Plains Burned

By Kyle McConnell

March 6, 2017

On a Monday morning in eastern Colorado, a spark from a welding torch ignited dry cornfield stubble. Within hours, 32,546 acres burned. The fire moved at 70 miles per hour.

This is the story of what happened next—told by the people who lived it.
