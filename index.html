The Logan Fire

When the Plains Burned

By Kyle McConnell

March 6, 2017

On a Monday morning in eastern Colorado, a spark from a welding torch ignited dry cornfield stubble. Within hours, 32,546 acres burned. The fire moved at 70 miles per hour.

This is the story of what happened next—told by the people who lived it.

Week One: The Fire

March 6-12, 2017

Day 1 — Monday, March 6

8:30 AM
Cole's Warning

Cole arrives at Bruce's farm, preps the water truck. "Might want to get that water truck ready with this wind."

~10:00 AM
First Smoke

Bruce sees smoke rolling over the hill. Posts to Facebook: "All prayers needed."

11:30 AM
The Spark

A welding torch ignites dry cornfield stubble. Wind gusting to 60 mph. The fire begins.

~12:00 PM
Fire Jumps I-76

The interstate is no barrier. Schools evacuate: Caliche, Fleming, Haxtun.

3:00 PM
State Takes Over

State assumes command of the Mack 10 radio channel. Local coordination disrupted.

Afternoon
The Cattle

185 head lost. Luke and Matt put down over 100 injured animals.

Evening
Houses Burn

Jared Burke's house catches fire. Help arrives 5 minutes too late.

Days 2-7

Day 1 Night
Glowing Earth

Fighting embers past midnight. The whole countryside like a thousand campfires.

Day 2 — 5:00 AM
Fire Restarts

Brian Kane's place catches fire. Luke races there with 1600 gallons of water.

Day 2 Morning
Tracy Defies Orders

Fleming Fire's Tracy O'Brien skips the state safety briefing. Stays on the line.

Day 2
The Wave and Nod

Sean Werner at the state roadblock lets the water truck through. No words needed.

Days 3-5
Mop-Up

Volunteers bring food to the fire hall. Farmers patrol for hot spots. Community mobilizes.

End of Week
Assessment

32,546 acres burned. 23 miles traveled in 8 hours. Dozens of families affected.

About This Project

Bruce Tucker

Fourth-generation farmer from Haxtun, Colorado. Lost 185 head of cattle in the fire—his kids' college funds. He and his neighbors rebuilt together.

Luke Harper

Bruce's cousin. Marine. Five tours in Iraq. The one you want beside you when things go bad. Witness to everything.
